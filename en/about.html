Our Story

Built by Athletes, For Athletes

FuelBox started in 2022 when our founders — two competitive athletes and a certified nutritionist — got tired of spending hours researching, comparing, and buying supplements every month.

They knew there had to be a better way. A single box with everything you need, curated by experts, delivered monthly, and at a fraction of the retail cost. That idea became FuelBox.

Today, we serve over 50,000 subscribers across the country. Every product in our boxes is lab-tested, sourced from trusted brands, and selected to deliver real results — whether you're a weekend warrior or a competitive athlete.

Lab-Tested Quality

Community First

Results-Driven
